## X thread: Exception management is the product

Route optimization gets the demo. Exception management gets used.

A dispatcher rarely needs software to celebrate the 87 deliveries that went as planned. She needs to know why twelve did not, which three can still be recovered, and who already promised the customer a new time.

The useful product questions are plain:

1. Can the driver report a problem in under thirty seconds?
2. Can the depot see it before the customer calls?
3. Does the next shift inherit the decision, not a mystery?

We improved one workflow last year by removing a dashboard and sending a structured WhatsApp prompt. Less software, better operation.
