# Community code of conduct

Be direct, curious, and respectful. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, dox, impersonate, spam, or exploit another contributor or network participant. Keep private reports and personal data out of public issues.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect people, credentials, the service, or the project. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome; retaliation and restriction evasion are not.

Report conduct concerns privately using the live service’s configured support or security contact.
